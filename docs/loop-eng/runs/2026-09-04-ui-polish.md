## Two decisions worth reviewing

**The tab bar is a component, not an expo-router `Tabs` navigator.** Moving `index`/`area`/`history`
into a `(tabs)` group renames three live routes and trips the Metro-misses-a-new-route-directory
hazard on Windows. Same bar, same behaviour, zero route churn. If the app ever needs real per-tab
state, promote it then.

**The officer surface is reached from History, not from a fourth tab.** A government dashboard
sitting beside History says the two audiences are peers, and the design rests on them not being.
But hiding it on `/board` failed a real user: John opened the app after three weeks, found three
flows, and concluded the officer screens did not exist. That was the wiring decision failing, not
his misreading.

## The map, specifically

John called the circles and bordered areas childish and said the officer's insight was unclear.
Both were one defect: **the map drew ornament, not data.** A 410 m hollow ring and eight outlined
rectangles — five of which held no data and were outlined anyway — and not one of those shapes ever
stated a number.

Now: one pill per hot block carrying that block's own count (B3 5 · B4 5 · B5 4, summing to the
cluster's 14), counted geometrically from the same coordinates the dots are drawn from, so pill and
dots cannot disagree. Behind each, a halo whose diameter goes as `sqrt(count/max)` — the eye
compares circle AREA, and scaling diameter linearly is the tell that a map was drawn rather than
projected. This is the Airbnb pattern in `docs/design/inspiration/mapcluster-1.png`, the oracle we
had all along and had not followed.

## One defect I introduced and caught

Shortening the area answer produced *"Aedes confirmed **nearby** in the last 72 hours"* — and
`nearby` is banned outright by specs §2. The language table exists precisely to catch this, and it
was breached by the pass whose job was cutting words. Reverted, and both languages swept. The sweep
also found a pre-existing `No cluster · monitoring` on the officer surface, now `watch`.
